# Plaid integration removed

Connector no longer offers Plaid bank connections or banking actions. Updating to this version preserves your other saved connections, developer settings, and AI access keys.

Removing the integration does not revoke existing bank-side authorizations or cancel a Plaid subscription. If you previously linked a bank, review and remove that access through Plaid or your bank as needed.

Grok Bot’s first-party Finance connector is a separate service. Removing Plaid from Connector does not change a Finance connection configured directly in Grok Bot.
